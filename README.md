# The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email

A bookkeeping service pitches a busy two-van plumber in one 120-word cold email.
`main.py` writes the email to `/output/entry.txt`, subject line first.

## The entry

The judge plays a plumber who reads email on a phone between jobs. The email
shows it knows his week, keeps the offer small and priced, and ends with one
easy ask. It runs 117 words in the body and 688 characters overall, under the
800-character gate.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Entry text is judged exactly as submitted, so the subject line is the first
line of the file.
